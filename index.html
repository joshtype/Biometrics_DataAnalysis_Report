About this project

This website presents analyses of user perceptions toward biometric modalities and examines stregnth of corrleation with acceptance of a modality vs experience and vs concern over privacy. Data analyses were conducted as part of a larger report on human-computer interaction (HCI) and biometric acceptance arguing that increased user experience via more user-friendly biometric HCI would improve user acceptance of such biometrics.

The dataset used for analyses was generated from a survey conducted for a paper focusing on medical biometrics, "Perspectives on facial images, DNA and other biometrics" (Wagner, 2021). It was publicly hosted at Synapse (DOI: 10.7303/SYN25618565).

The survey recorded several demographic variables with metrics for measurings experience across six biometric modalities, acceptability per modality, acceptability across a number of biomedical applications, trust in facial recognition and DNA modalities across several entities, and general concern over privacy infringement. Variables used by present research included demographics, experience, acceptability per modality, acceptability per modality sgrouped-by demographics, and privacy concern.
