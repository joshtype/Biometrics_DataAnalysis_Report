About this project

This website is a research artifact, allowing users to interact with visualizations generated from a report on the impact of biometric human-computer interaction (HCI) and user acceptance of biometrics. Analyses of survey data* were conducted as part of research on the impact of human- computer interaction (HCI) on user acceptance of a biometric system. Observing data trends led to the hypothesis that statistically significant correlation exists between user experience with a modality and user acceptance of the modality. This, along with another potential correlative factor found to be neglible, were tested via Spearman. This showed weak-positive statistical correlation between experience and user acceptance.

*The dataset was generated from a survey conducted for a paper focusing on medical biometrics: "Perspectives on facial images, DNA, and other biometrics" (Wagner, 2021)**. The dataset was publicly available from host site: Synapse (DOI: 10.7303/SYN25618565).

**The survey recorded several demographic variables with metrics for measurings experience across six biometric modalities, acceptability per modality, acceptability across a number of biomedical applications, trust in facial recognition and DNA usage across key industries, and general concern over privacy infringement.
